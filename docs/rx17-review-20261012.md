## Repairs and invariants

- Gold forms additively over opaque, beveled gunmetal after each plate seats.
  The regenerated public cut-out retains its revised driver and assembly lighting;
  the unformed-channel floor is repaired without introducing transparent holes.
  The already-complete driver is excluded from the generated gold-cell allocation,
  eliminating 546 phantom entries (9521 initialized, drawable cells remain).
- Light separation now has distinct snapshot, budgeted row, commit, and working-
  atlas tasks. It uses the old per-pixel arithmetic unchanged. Tests compare every
  RGBA byte over the real 1024×866 region (886,784 pixels), seeded colors, alpha,
  hue boundaries, and multiple slicing patterns.
- Low idle work and high-priority intent share one preparation and cursor. A
  promoted job invalidates stale callbacks. Partial layers never publish; a live
  call's formed atlas cannot be reset by a resumed background job. Source changes,
  context loss, failed commits, and raw fallback remain retryable. The synchronous
  ZENITH API is retained, including safe compatibility with width-less test data.
- The cooperative loop has a 6 ms budget. Native readback/commit and one row are
  indivisible, so this is not a guarantee that every browser task takes ≤6 ms.
- Decoded image readiness no longer truncates an unfinished plan to 120 ms.
  Decode/import/plan share a finite, cancellable **1800 ms preparation budget**;
  an already-prepared promise resolves immediately. The renderer chunk has its
  own bound, and two pre-gate frames yield the UI: click-to-cover total is not
  promised to fit into 1800 ms. The call clock begins after preparation, not while
  it runs. History, hiding, pagehide and disposal invalidate stale navigation.
- One-second idle reservation, route-change rescheduling, no opening-route warmup,
  hidden-page inactivity, simultaneous low-priority image starts, abortable
  background requests, and missing-only retries remain covered by tests.
